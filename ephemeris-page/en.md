## Reading this page

The positions table lists geocentric longitudes for the Sun, Moon and
planets at local midnight, with each body's motion and sign. The agenda
below it times the day's events — ingresses, lunar aspects and Moon
void-of-course periods — in your own timezone. Rise and set times need
your coordinates; without a saved location the page falls back to a
default city and says so.

Everything here is computed in your browser from the Swiss Ephemeris.
The dignity almanac — exaltations, triplicities, terms and faces across
a 33-day window — lives on the dashboard, and eclipse search has its own
page. Nothing you enter leaves your device.
