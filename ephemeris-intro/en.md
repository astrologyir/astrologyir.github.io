## What this ephemeris shows

Every figure on this page is computed in your browser from the Swiss
Ephemeris. Nothing is stored on a server and no account is needed.

**Planetary positions.** Geocentric longitudes for the Sun, Moon and the
classical planets, refreshed continuously. Switch between tropical and
sidereal zodiac in Settings; the whole page follows your choice.

**Dignity almanac.** A rolling window of planetary dignities — exaltation,
triplicity, term and face — running from three days back to thirty days
ahead, so a coming change is visible before it lands.

**Day agenda.** Every event for the selected day with an exact time in your
own timezone: sign and degree ingresses, lunar aspects, Moon
void-of-course periods, and the Sun's rise, transit, set and twilight
bands.

**Sun and Moon.** Rise, set, twilight boundaries, current phase,
illuminated fraction and apparent diameter for your coordinates.

**Calendars.** The same day expressed in Jalali, Gregorian and Hijri.

## Why it runs locally

The calculations are small enough to run in a browser, so the page stays
fast and keeps working offline. Nothing you enter — location, date or birth
details — leaves your device. Use the date picker to look ahead, and set a
home location in Settings so the almanac and rise/set times match where
you are.
